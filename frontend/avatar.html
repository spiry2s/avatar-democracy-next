Set up your Avatar

Your Avatar can record a position from what you set here. You can change the setup or override a later vote. Three parts: a coarse compass, your own words, and people you choose on specific issues.

1 · Political compass

Not saved

A coarse compass is not a full political identity and not a validated psychometric test. Nothing here is a scientific score. It is only a fallback the Avatar can use when you have not written values or named a delegate for that part of a bill. Unanswered axes sit in the middle. You can change or clear every answer, then save.

Choose any answers you want. Nothing is selected for you.

2 · Values, in your own words

Free text. Say what you want the Avatar to follow, in your own words. Nothing is filled in for you. The more specific you are, the less the compass is used as a fallback.

3 · Per-issue delegates

0

On specific issues, follow someone you trust. Your Avatar approximates how they'd vote from their public positions. Adding a delegate has a cooling-off period before it takes effect; revoking is instant.
